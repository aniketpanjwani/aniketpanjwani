## Hi, I'm Aniket 👋

I'm an economist (PhD, Northwestern) who builds software and teaches knowledge workers to get the most out of AI.

- **[AI Economist](https://aieconomist.io):** I teach agentic coding with Claude Code and Codex to economists. I've trained 500+ economists in group and individual sessions, including MIT's economics department, [Opportunity Insights at Harvard](https://aieconomist.io/case-studies/opportunity-insights), Deutsche Bundesbank, and the UK's Office for National Statistics.
- **[Institute for Progress](https://ifp.org):** I'm the first member of technical staff. I build software that magnifies and scales IFP's work, develop its AI strategy, and train its researchers to use AI.
- **[EconNow](https://econ.now):** a directory of economics job market candidates, funded by an [Emergent Ventures grant](https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2025/12/emergent-ventures-winners-50th-cohort.html) in 2025.
- **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@aniketapanjwani):** agentic coding tutorials for a general audience and for economists (10k+ subscribers).

Before this, I led teams of ML engineers and data scientists at Zelle, working on systems with terabytes of data from hundreds of financial institutions. As Director of AI & ML at Payslice, I built the risk model and its infrastructure from scratch. My research has been published in the *Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization* and *Information Economics and Policy*.
